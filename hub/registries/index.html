Clinical Registries

Prospective, IRB-approved limited data sets maintained by the Neuroscience Research Department.

About the registries

Governance

Each registry operates under an umbrella IRB protocol as a limited data set. Access is role-based: clinical and research staff may enter data; students, IRB and administrative staff have read-only access. Every view, query, edit and export is written to the audit log.

Inclusion

BTR — adults and children with a histologically or radiographically confirmed CNS tumour treated at Saint Luke's. TBI — traumatic brain injury, subdural / epidural haematoma, traumatic SAH and skull fracture admissions. FNR — epilepsy surgery, deep brain stimulation, neuromodulation for pain and spasticity.

Data dictionary & manuals

Variable definitions, coding rules (WHO CNS5, GCS, Marshall, GOS-E, Engel, UPDRS, VAS, Ashworth) and entry SOPs live in the documentation library.

Open Registry Manuals
PHI reminder. Never record names, MRNs, dates of birth, addresses, phone numbers or free-text that could identify a patient. Use the study ID and the approved key held by the research office to re-identify when clinically necessary.
