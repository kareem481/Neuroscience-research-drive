Databases & registries

Request data

The department maintains three IRB-governed clinical registries. They are internal resources: access is limited to approved Saint Luke's investigators and study staff, and every use is tied to an approved protocol.

Brain Tumor Registry

Longitudinal records for patients with primary and metastatic central nervous system tumors, capturing surgery, radiation and systemic therapy, imaging and volumetric measurements, RANO assessments, tumor board decisions and biobank specimens.

  • Progression and follow-up tracking
  • Clinical-trial enrollment and research cohorts

Trauma / TBI Registry

Traumatic brain injury and cranial trauma (including subdural and epidural hematoma and skull fracture), with intracranial pressure readings, imaging, complications and standardized outcome assessments.

  • Neurocritical care physiology
  • Functional outcome follow-up

Functional Neurosurgery Registry

Epilepsy surgery, deep brain stimulation, pain and spasticity procedures, with device programming history, seizure logs, medications, neurophysiology and validated outcome instruments.

  • Device and programming timeline
  • Seizure and symptom diaries

Registry data never leave the institution without IRB approval and a data-use agreement. Learn how data requests work on the Services page, or contact the research office.
